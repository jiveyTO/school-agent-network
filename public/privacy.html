ParentLoop Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

ParentLoop helps parents organize school information in shared hubs, and lets a family’s own AI agent prepare a nightly check filtered to their child.

Information we collect

  • Account information: when you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address.
  • Family profile: your name and the children you add (first name, grade, and school or team placements), so the evening check can be filtered to your child.
  • Hub content: materials you or other hub members add — newsletters, notices, photos of posters, pasted text, and forwarded emails — and the facts extracted from them.
  • Agent access: if you connect an AI agent, we store a hashed access token. The raw token is never stored.

How we use information

We use this information only to operate ParentLoop: running hubs, extracting and verifying facts, preparing your family’s evening check, sending account-recovery and (if you enable it) nightly check emails, and keeping the service secure.

AI processing

Hub materials are processed with Meta’s Model API to extract structured facts. Every extracted fact must carry a verbatim quote from its source material, and facts whose quotes do not check out are dropped.

Sharing

Hub members can see other members’ parent names and roles and the materials shared in the hub. Children’s names and grades are never shown to hub members. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Email

We send email through Resend, for sign-in recovery and nightly checks.

Demo

The public demo uses fictional data only.

Retention and deletion

You may ask us to delete your account and family data at any time by emailing jtigas@gmail.com.

Children

ParentLoop accounts are created by parents or guardians. Information about children is entered by their parent or guardian.

Changes

We will update this page when this policy changes.
